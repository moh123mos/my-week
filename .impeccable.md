## Design Context

### Users
The author, for personal daily use on their own phone (Arabic, RTL). Quick one-handed check-ins after prayers and through the day; weekly review of habits and "beginnings".

### Brand Personality
Calm, spiritual, private. Quiet confidence; the tap-to-check grid is the hero.

### Aesthetic Direction
"A notebook under moonlight." Night indigo background with ruled lines instead of cards, and a single warm moonlight accent. Aref Ruqaa for the wordmark, titles and handwritten notes; Cairo for the interface. Prayer colours come from the sky at that time (cool dawn, sunny noon, amber afternoon, copper sunset, indigo night).

The signature is the moon: the logo is a crescent completed by seven dots (the days), weekly progress is a moon that grows into a full moon, and "the week's rhythm" is seven small moons.

Do not use gradient text, side-stripe borders, glass or blur, glowing shadows, neon violet/cyan, or red for missed days.

### Design Principles
1. Today first: the current day and its checkboxes are the easiest things to reach.
2. Thumb-first: touch targets >= 44px, sheets anchored to the bottom.
3. Above the fold: summary is compact so habit rows appear immediately.
4. Same information architecture on every screen size.
